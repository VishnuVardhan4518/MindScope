About MindScope

Understanding student wellbeing through data, machine learning, and thoughtful design.

STUDENT WELLNESS × MACHINE LEARNING

Turning student data into meaningful insights.

MindScope is a student mental-health prediction project designed to explore how lifestyle, academic, digital, and wellbeing-related factors can be used as inputs to a machine-learning prediction system.

01

02

06 / LIMITATIONS

What the system doesn't claim.

01

A prediction from the model is not a clinical diagnosis.

02

The system does not establish that one lifestyle factor causes a mental-health outcome.

03

Recommendations are general wellness guidance rather than individualized medical treatment.

04

The model's output depends on the quality and characteristics of the data used to train it.

About MindScope

MindScope is an educational machine-learning project focused on student wellbeing. Its predictions and resources are intended for informational purposes and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical or mental-health assessment.
